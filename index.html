Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about turning your paper designs into reality with Ma Aashapuri Furniture.

Yes, absolutely! Over 40% of our clients start with pencil sketches, architectural floor plans, or napkin diagrams. Our senior architects convert your 2D sketches into sub-millimeter AutoCAD drawings and 3D photorealistic walkthroughs before manufacturing in our in-house 40,000 sq.ft plant.

Because 90% of your modular woodwork, kitchen cabinetry, and custom wardrobes are pre-fabricated in our climate-controlled CNC factory, on-site execution is clean and rapid. Once design approvals and site civil clearances are complete, we guarantee handover within 45 days.

We use authentic IS 710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Plywood, seasoned Burma Teakwood, natural smoked oak veneers, and Italian Calacatta marble. All hardware fittings (hinges, soft-close channels, lift-ups) are sourced from Blum (Austria) and Hafele (Germany) with lifetime operational warranties.

Unlike aggregator platforms that subcontract work to 3rd-party vendors adding 30-40% middleman margins, Ma Aashapuri owns its direct manufacturing plant and master carpentry atelier. You get genuine factory-direct pricing with superior European hardware and zero hidden charges.

Our 10-year warranty covers all woodwork delamination, termite resistance, structural bending, and carcass durability. Furthermore, all German Blum and Hafele hinges, runners, and lift-up hardware come with lifetime manufacturer warranty coverage.
