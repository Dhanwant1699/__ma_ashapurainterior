Our Signature Methodology

The Paper to Reality Journey

How we take your hand sketches, architect blueprints, or CAD files and transform them into exquisite, hand-finished real-life living spaces in 45 days.

01

Paper Design Submission

You share your architectural floor plans, pencil sketches, Pinterest mood boards, or AutoCAD DWG layouts with our senior design team.

Deliverable: Design Brief & Scope Audit
02

3D Laser Site Scanning

Our site engineers use sub-millimeter precision laser measurement tools at your property to map beam offsets, wall angles, and plumbing clearances.

Deliverable: 100% Accurate As-Built CAD
03

Photorealistic 3D Renders

We convert your 2D paper design into immersive 3D virtual walkthroughs, with exact lighting, wood textures, marble veining, and upholstery simulations.

Deliverable: 4K Renders & Material Samples
04

In-House CNC Woodcraft

Your furniture is precision-cut at our 40,000 sq.ft factory using German Homag CNC machinery, IS 710 Marine Plywood, and hand-carved solid teak accents.

Deliverable: Factory Pre-Assembled Units
05

Zero-Dust Site Installation

Modular modules are delivered with protective wrapping and assembled on-site in days, avoiding weeks of chaotic dust and noisy carpentry in your home.

Deliverable: 45-Day Move-In Guarantee
06

10-Yr Warranty Handover

Final quality inspection, deep cleaning, and formal handover of your dream home complete with your 10-year warranty certificate and lifetime hardware support.

Deliverable: Warranty & Care Kit
